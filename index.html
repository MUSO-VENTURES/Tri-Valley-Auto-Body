Our Story

Three Generations, One Standard.

Tri-Valley Auto Body opened its doors on 1st Street in 1980. Four and a half decades later, we're still on the same street, still family-run, and still standing behind every repair with a lifetime workmanship warranty.

What's changed is the equipment: computerized frame measuring, ADAS calibration, and a down-draft paint booth that lets us match any factory finish. What hasn't changed is who answers the phone when you call.
